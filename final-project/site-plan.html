Site Plan

Site Name

Fresko

I selected the name "Fresko" (a play on the word "Fresco" which means "Fresh" in Spanish) because the primary goal of the application is to keep food fresh and reduce waste. It is short, easy to remember, and feels modern and clean. It aligns with the concept of having a fresh perspective on home resource management and self-reliance.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Fresko is to provide families and individuals with a digital stewardship tool to manage their home food inventory. The site aims to promote self-reliance by helping users track expiration dates to prevent food waste and save money.

The application will allow users to quickly scan or input groceries, visualize their pantry stock through a color-coded "traffic light" system (Green, Yellow, Red), and provide immediate recipe suggestions for ingredients that are about to spoil. It transforms the pantry from a storage space into an active resource.

Scenarios

The following are questions that a typical user might ask when visiting the site:

  • Scenario 1: "I have a lot of cans and dry goods in the back of my pantry. How do I know which ones need to be used first so I don't have to throw them away later?"
  • Scenario 2: "I have chicken and spinach in the fridge that expire tomorrow, but I don't know what to make. What recipe can I cook right now using these ingredients?"
  • Scenario 3: "I want to be more self-reliant with my budget. How can I see exactly what food I already have at home before I go shopping for more?"

Typography

Headings: Archivo Black

I have chosen Archivo Black for headings. It is a heavyweight sans-serif font that provides the bold, impactful look required for the Neobrutalist design.

Example H1: Manage Your Pantry Efficiently

Body Text: System Sans-Serif

I am using the system's default sans-serif font for body text to ensure maximum legibility and performance, keeping the interface clean and functional.

Example Body Text: Fresko helps you keep track of your groceries and their expiration dates, so you can reduce waste and save money. With a simple color-coded system, you'll always know what to use first.
